About Ridgewood

A Different Kind of Design-Build Firm

Ridgewood Design Build is a boutique luxury residential firm that operates at the intersection of architecture and workmanship. We begin where most builders don't — identifying exceptional homesites, then designing residences that respond to each property's unique character and topography.

From the greater Raleigh area's most coveted in-fill lots to the pristine coastline of North Carolina's barrier islands, each Ridgewood residence is a singular expression of site, client, and vision.
